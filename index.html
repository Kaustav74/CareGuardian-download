How to install

  1. Tap a download button on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps (Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, then open CareGuardian and sign in or create an account.
Important: Google Play re-signs the app. If you installed the APK, uninstall it before installing CareGuardian from Google Play.
